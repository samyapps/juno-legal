Juno: AI Parent Assistant – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

EnglishTürkçe

1. Who we are

Juno: AI Parent Assistant ("Juno", "the app") is published by Samy Apps, operated by Azizenur Albayrak, Istanbul, Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.
Contact: info@samyapps.com

2. Data we collect

a) Account data

When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email address (or an Apple "Hide My Email" relay address), profile picture link if provided by Google, and a unique user ID. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.

b) Baby care records you enter

Feeding, sleep, diaper changes, medicine names and doses, temperature readings, growth measurements (weight, height), notes, and the dates and times of these entries. This may include health-related information about your child. These records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so they are available after reinstalling or on a new device.

c) AI assistant

When you ask the AI assistant a question, the text of your question and your app language are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer. We do not store the content of your questions or the answers on our servers. We only store how many questions you asked on the current day, to apply the daily limit. Please do not include names or other identifying details in your questions.

d) Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. Our subscription provider RevenueCat receives your purchase information and user ID so that we can unlock Premium features on your devices.

e) Data that stays on your device

Agenda/calendar entries, milestones (including any photos you attach – these are not uploaded), favourite sounds, reminders, and language/theme settings are stored only on your device.

f) Technical data

Our cloud provider automatically records technical logs (for example IP address, time of request, errors) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are kept for a limited time.

We do not show ads, do not sell your data, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Why we use your data and legal bases

  • To provide the app (account, syncing your records, AI answers, Premium access) – performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Health-related information about your child – your explicit consent, which you give by entering it (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a); KVKK Art. 6). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the entries or your account.
  • Security and abuse prevention (e.g. daily AI limits, logs) – our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Legal obligations – where the law requires us to keep or disclose data (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).

4. Service providers

We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google LLC – Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Gemini API (servers in the United States).
  • Apple Inc. – Sign in with Apple, App Store payments.
  • Google Play – payments on Android.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. – subscription management (United States).

5. International transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the United States. Transfers outside Türkiye and the EU/EEA are made on the basis of the providers' standard contractual clauses and other safeguards permitted by KVKK Art. 9 and GDPR Chapter V.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data and baby care records: until you delete them or your account.
  • AI usage counter: replaced daily; deleted with your account.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from our active systems immediately. Copies in our providers' logs or backups are deleted according to their schedules, normally within 30 days.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple/Google under their own policies and as required by law.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email info@samyapps.com from the address you use to sign in. We respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local data protection authority in the EU/EEA.

8. Deleting your account

In the app, tap the account icon (top right) → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all baby care records. If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription – please cancel it in your store account settings.

9. Children

Juno is intended for adults (parents and caregivers). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly allow children to create accounts. Information about a baby is entered and controlled by the parent or caregiver.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your records is restricted to your own account. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Not medical advice

Juno and its AI assistant provide general information only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, inform you in the app.

13. Contact

Samy Apps – Azizenur Albayrak, Istanbul, Türkiye · info@samyapps.com
